                      Good to know

                      • 🎧Wear headphones. Every sound is placed in 3D around you, and button clicks come from where you tap.
                      • 🌱Write today's intention under the timer (I). After a session, tap 📝 to note what you got done; tap the pencil next to any session in Stats to add or change its note.
                      • 🏷️Add #tags to task names, like "Read chapter 3 #school". Stats then shows how your focus splits between them. Tap any day in the heatmap to see its sessions.
                      • 👆Tap a task to count focus sessions towards it. Drag the grip to reorder, double-click a name to rename it. On a phone, swipe a task right to tick it off or left to delete it.
                      • 🎛️Tap several sounds to mix them, or pick a ready-made mix. In the room, drag each sound around you, press orbit to send it circling, or drag your own head to turn around.
                      • 🪟Pop the timer out (P) into a small window that stays on top while you work in other apps.
                      • 🌊Prefer to stop when you lose steam? Tap the length under the timer and pick ∞ Flowtime. The timer counts up, and when you stop you earn a break worth a fifth of the time you worked.
                      • ⚡Mind wandered? Tap Distracted (D), note the thought as a task if it matters, and carry on. Rate each session when it ends.
                      • 🧘Zen mode hides everything except the timer. During breaks, follow the breathing orb.
                      • 🎯Set a daily goal in Settings. Your streak, heatmap, history and achievements are in Stats, where you can also share an image of your day.
                      • 📲Install Tempo from your browser's menu to use it like an app, even offline.
                      • 🔒Everything you do here stays on this device.

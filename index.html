      Good to know

      • 🎧Wear headphones. Every sound is placed in 3D around you, and button clicks come from where you tap.
      • 👆Tap a task to count focus sessions towards it. Drag the grip to reorder, double-click a name to rename it.
      • 🧘Zen mode hides everything except the timer. During breaks, follow the breathing orb.
      • 🎯Set a daily goal in Settings. Your streak, heatmap and achievements are in Stats.
      • 📲Install Tempo from your browser's menu to use it like an app, even offline.
      • 🔒Everything you do here stays on this device.
